Our Model

We bring together the world’s leading scientists, institutions, and industry partners to make sure promising immuno-oncology discoveries move further, faster — from early investigation all the way to real-world patient impact.

Institutes

Collaborative Science to Support Progress

Parker Institutes comprises a network of over a dozen centers and labs and hundreds of individual investigators relentlessly pursuing the field’s most promising science, not in silos, but together. Our long-term relationships encourage continuity and collaboration, and our early-career awards guarantee a deep bench for the long haul.

Research Focus Areas

We define the frontiers and breakthroughs with the most potential, and create the conditions for researchers to explore them freely. Across our Focus Areas, Parker Institute scientists are making paradigm-shifting and practice-changing discoveries, resulting in hundreds of published research papers per year, as well as the IP that drives our Innovations and BioVentures work.

Collaborative Science to Support Progress

A fundamental challenge in cancer research is known as the “gap of death.” It’s the space between findings and funding for the next steps outside of the lab. We collaborate with technology transfer teams at each Center, and help navigate complex IP and patent processes to keep moving forward. At Parker, all of the innovations developed at our Institutes result in owned IP that can be licensed, combined, and delivered where it’s most needed.

→Learn about our IP capabilities

600+
Inventions
and Counting

Since our founding, Parker Institutes have generated over 600 inventions, spanning unique screening techniques, engineering and armoring technologies, and machine learning to power the next wave of innovation. The most promising of these inventions have been licensed or optioned to companies dedicated to further developing and commercializing these technologies and possible therapeutics.
