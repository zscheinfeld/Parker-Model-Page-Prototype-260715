Our Model

No Brakes. Only Breakthroughs

People with cancer have no time to waste, and neither do we. That’s why Parker’s end-to-end approach removes barriers, bridges gaps, and actively drives ideas forward.

We turn a stop-and-start process into a full-speed-ahead pipeline from lab to patient — so breakthroughs don’t get stuck, and more of them actually save lives, sooner.

Here’s how.

Our Model

Ten years ago, we decided the promise of immunotherapy couldn’t wait. With some of the world’s leading scientists, we began to chart a strategic path toward patient impact, within — and outside — the system.

An Unparalleled Network

Parker Institutes partners with top academic centers to support leading-edge research, including the long-shot ideas most funders shy away from.

We define the scientific frontiers with the most potential to reach patients, and create the conditions for researchers to explore them freely and collaboratively. This results in hundreds of research publications per year.

From IP to Patients

Parker Innovations helps bridge the gap between lab and commercialization — so more discoveries have a chance to make a difference in the lives of more patients, more quickly.

As stewards of all intellectual property generated across our network, our Innovations team maintains a bird’s-eye view of the most exciting emerging science to develop and license to biotech and pharma partners.

Investing in
the Best

We actively assist in building companies that keep promising science moving forward: recruiting leadership, leading early funding rounds, and bringing the right investors to the table. Most of the time, these companies are using IP developed by Parker Institutes.

Any proceeds from the success of these enterprises are ultimately recycled back into Parker, and the research we fund.

Vital Connections

What happens among and between our activities and partners is truly special, taking innovation to patient impact with more creativity and speed.
